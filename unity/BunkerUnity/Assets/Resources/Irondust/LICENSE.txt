The original white colour atlas was resized to 1024 x 1024 and encoded as JPEG.
Models were converted from the original ASCII FBX 6.1 to OBJ, preserving local
geometry, polygon normals, and UVs; Blender Z-up coordinates were rotated to Y-up.
This asset is under evaluation, not a finished bunker or gameplay scene.
